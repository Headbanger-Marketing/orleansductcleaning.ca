Reading what a workmanship warranty covers before work is agreed

A proposal may list tasks without saying which of them carry written workmanship terms, how long those terms last or what would end them. Read each task line in the proposal for your Orleans home and mark the ones with terms attached: a resealed joint, a reconnected dryer vent, a reinstalled HRV core, a closed access opening. List anything the terms ask you to keep, such as the job record, so that each line can be answered by a provider who responds.

  1. Return grilles

    Where room air is drawn back toward the furnace.

  2. Return ducts

    The runs leading from each grille to the furnace.

  3. Furnace blower and coil

    The blower, evaporator coil and cabinet.

  4. Supply trunk

    The largest duct carrying air away from the furnace.

  5. Branch runs

    Smaller ducts feeding each room.

  6. Supply registers

    The floor, wall or ceiling vents in each room.

Separate runs to ask about:Dryer ventHRV core and screensBathroom fan ductsRange hood exhaust

  • Terms for each task

    Note which tasks in the proposal name written workmanship terms and which are silent, so the provider can fill the gaps in writing.

  • Conditions that end terms

    Write down any condition the proposal says would end the terms, such as later work by someone else or a missing record.

  • Records you will keep

    List the job record, photographs and invoices you can hold, since terms may depend on producing them later.

Questions About Your Orleans Request

Does Orleans Duct Cleaning do the cleaning itself, and did it send my proposal?

No. A request sent here is reviewed by our enquiry team, which may pass it on to a provider. This is an enquiry website, not a contractor: it has no crew, office or vehicles, and it does not issue terms of any kind. Before anything is arranged, that provider must identify themselves, confirm coverage of your address and set out their own written terms.

How is my Orleans request handled after I send it, and where does that stop?

You agree the scope in writing with a responding provider, if one is found, once they contact you separately. Sending puts your details and any proposal in front of our enquiry team for review. It does not book work, name a provider, confirm coverage, set a price or create terms of any kind.

How do I read workmanship terms in a duct cleaning proposal for my Orleans home?

Look for terms written against each task rather than one line for the whole job. Check how long they apply, what the provider says would end them and what record you must keep. If the proposal is silent, ask the responding provider to add the terms in writing. Nothing on this site shows that any terms exist, so treat each one as something to confirm. Keep a copy of every page with the date it reached you.

What should I send if I have no proposal yet but want terms explained first?

Describe the system and the tasks you expect, such as ducts, a dryer vent or an HRV, and say you want the written terms for each task stated up front. Photographs of registers, labels and the outside hood help. The responding provider can then quote task by task, and you can compare their terms before agreeing. Keep a copy of everything you send.

Can one Orleans request ask for terms on both the dryer vent and the ducts?

Yes, one request can list several services, and each should be asked about separately. A dryer vent and a duct system are different runs, so terms for one may not extend to the other. Ask the provider to state which tasks each set of terms covers and keep that statement with the rest of the paperwork. If you plan to agree to the two at different times, say so in the request.

What if a provider says the terms would end if someone else touches the work?

Ask for that condition in writing, with the specific work it refers to. A vague statement is hard to rely on later. Find out whether routine actions such as a filter change count, and who decides. Note the answer in your request so the responding provider can address it before the scope is agreed. Photographs of the finished work, taken before anyone else works nearby, show its condition at handover.

Who can accept written terms on a rented space in Orleans?

Usually the person who may authorize the work, which could be an owner, landlord, property manager or condominium corporation rather than the occupant. Ask who must sign and who holds the record afterwards. Questions about what a lease or contract requires belong with the landlord or a lawyer, not with this site. Ask that the signer be named in the written scope.

Does a record kept after cleaning matter for terms on my Orleans home?

It may. Terms can refer to a job record, so ask what it should contain: the tasks done, the locations reached, photographs and any finding left alone. Keep the record with invoices and the proposal in one place. If the provider's terms depend on it, ask for the wording in writing before the work starts. Ask whether the terms run from the date of the work or the date of the invoice.
